The deposit is a settlement instrument, not a charge — but it lives in pricing_components_fact. Order A's charges come to 5,360,842, which is exactly what it captured minus the deposit it released. Sum every component instead and you get 8,497,424 — the original captured amount, back again, because RefundDeposit equals the reversals minus the fee and cancels them out.

Order B shows the mirror: charges of 4,242,120, but every component summed gives 1,105,538 — the cash still due, not the order's value. Same table, two different meanings of "total". Either move both deposit lines to the payment layer, or tag them non-pricing and exclude them from every sum.
